# Food My Way go-to-market plan

Updated: September 26, 2026

## Product decision

- Brand: **Food My Way**
- First product promise: flexible recipes for picky eaters
- Primary domain: **foodmyway.app**
- Supporting acquisition domain: **PickyEaterCookbook.com**, redirected to a picky-eater landing path on the primary domain
- One-line positioning: **Start with foods you already like. Get a simple recipe you can swap, save, and make your way.**

This positioning avoids competing as a generic recipe generator. The wedge is reducing mealtime conflict and decision fatigue for picky adults, parents, and neurodivergent households.

## Offer and pricing

### Private beta

- Core generator and swaps: free
- Founding-member reservation: no charge until secure checkout and cloud accounts are ready

### First paid release

- Founding Member: **$29 one time**, limited to the first 250 customers
- Free: limited creations and saved recipes
- Plus: **$4.99/month or $39/year**
- Family, after multi-profile validation: **$59/year**

Do not advertise “lifetime” without defining it in checkout as the lifetime of the Food My Way premium product, not the buyer’s lifetime, and preserving the right to charge separately for materially different future products.

## What customers are buying

They are not buying recipes. They are buying:

1. fewer dinner arguments;
2. fewer abandoned ingredients and wasted groceries;
3. less decision fatigue;
4. a repeatable way to adapt one meal instead of cooking separate meals;
5. a judgment-free tool that starts with safe and familiar foods.

## Revenue path

At an average realized recurring revenue of roughly $4.25 per paying household per month:

- $10,000 monthly recurring revenue requires about **2,353 paying households**.
- $20,000 monthly recurring revenue requires about **4,706 paying households**.

This is achievable only through retention, not one viral post. The operating targets are:

- visitor → first recipe: 35% or better;
- first recipe → saved recipe: 25% or better;
- activated user → trial/founding interest: 8% or better;
- trial → paid: 20% or better;
- paid monthly churn: below 5%;
- annual-plan mix: above 55%;
- customer-acquisition cost: below $20 until retention is proven.

## Launch sequence

### Phase 1 — private proof

- Recruit 25 target households manually.
- Require each tester to make at least three recipes over seven days.
- Interview anyone who stops after one recipe.
- Collect permission before using any quote, name, image, or video publicly.
- Ask for a real $29 founding purchase only after secure checkout is live.

### Phase 2 — paid founding launch

- Open 250 founding memberships.
- Publish one short-form video daily for 30 days.
- Run weekly live demos using actual viewer ingredient suggestions.
- Add referral codes only after the first 25 organic purchases.
- Do not spend on ads until at least 30% of new users return within seven days.

### Phase 3 — recurring release

- Introduce Plus monthly and annual plans.
- Add household profiles, “never suggest,” weekly planning, and grocery lists.
- Publish searchable landing pages for picky adults, parents, sensory preferences, and weeknight meals.
- Test partnerships with occupational therapists, dietitians, parent creators, and neurodivergent creators without making medical claims.

## Market evidence reviewed

- Yuka publicly reports $11.88M in 2025 premium subscription revenue: https://yuka.io/en/independence/
- Yuka demonstrates a low annual consumer-food subscription price point around $15/year: https://yuka.io/en/premium-member/
- Mealime’s official Pro documentation validates nutrition, favorites, history, and recipe access as premium meal-planning features: https://support.mealime.com/article/79-mealime-pro

Competitor revenue proves willingness to pay for useful food decisions, not demand for this exact product. Food My Way must validate its own conversion and retention.

## Non-negotiable trust rules

- Never claim recipes are allergen-safe.
- Never diagnose ARFID, autism, sensory disorders, eating disorders, or nutritional deficiencies.
- Never tell a parent or adult to force exposure or restrict safe foods.
- Label nutrition as estimated.
- Do not invent testimonials, download counts, expert endorsements, or clinical outcomes.
- Do not add advertising that compromises the “no judgment” promise.

## Launch gates

The public paid launch does not open until all are true:

- custom domain and HTTPS verified;
- support email receives and sends successfully;
- Stripe product, price, checkout, refunds, and tax settings verified;
- real authentication and cloud deletion/export paths tested;
- privacy policy and terms reviewed with correct operator identity;
- analytics captures visit, generation, save, founding-interest, checkout, and purchase events;
- error monitoring is active;
- mobile Safari, mobile Chrome, desktop Chrome, and desktop Edge pass the critical flow;
- backup and rollback procedure is documented;
- at least 15 private testers complete the core flow without live assistance.
