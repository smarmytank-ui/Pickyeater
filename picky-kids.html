One flexible plan for a household with different preferences

Parents often know which foods are dependable but still face the nightly question: what can these ingredients become? Food My Way turns those starting points into a recipe, then lets you replace individual ingredients and save the household version that actually gets used.

A calmer weekly routine

  1. Name familiar proteins, starches, vegetables, or cheeses.
  2. Adjust the generated recipe for texture and household preferences.
  3. Save dependable meals to the Recipe Book.
  4. Combine the week into one grocery list instead of planning from scratch nightly.

Important limits

Does Food My Way guarantee allergy safety?

No. Always verify product labels, cross-contact risks, and ingredients yourself. Seek qualified guidance for allergies or medical nutrition.

Is the goal to force children to try foods?

No. Food My Way is a low-pressure organization tool. It does not provide behavioral, feeding, or medical treatment.
