# Food My Way support templates

Use these as starting points. Replace bracketed text and remove irrelevant lines before sending.

## First response

Subject: We received your Food My Way request

Hi [name],

Thanks for writing. I'm looking into [brief issue] and will follow up by [date or timeframe]. Please do not send card numbers, passwords, or medical information.

— Food My Way Support

## Purchase investigation

Subject: Food My Way purchase review

Hi [name],

I can check this without any card information. Please confirm the email used at Stripe Checkout and the approximate purchase date. If available, include the Stripe receipt number—but not a screenshot containing payment details.

— Food My Way Support

## Refund confirmation

Subject: Your Food My Way refund

Hi [name],

Your refund for [amount] was submitted through Stripe on [date]. Stripe estimates that it will appear according to the timeframe shown on your refund confirmation. Your founding access is now marked refunded.

— Food My Way Support

## Data-deletion acknowledgment

Subject: Food My Way privacy request

Hi [name],

We received your request concerning [founding-list / purchase / account] data. We'll verify ownership and respond by [date]. Recipes and diary entries stored only in your browser can be removed through Food My Way's data controls; clearing them cannot be undone.

— Food My Way Support

## Data-deletion completion

Subject: Food My Way privacy request completed

Hi [name],

We completed the requested deletion or anonymization on [date], except for records we must retain for [accounting / fraud prevention / legal obligation]. Those retained records are restricted to that purpose.

— Food My Way Support
