# Food My Way short-form content playbook

## Channel promise

**We turn picky-eater safe foods into one realistic meal—without judgment.**

Use `foodmyway.app` as the spoken and on-screen destination once the custom domain is live. Do not send public traffic to the protected preview URL.

## Repeatable video formats

### 1. Four safe foods → one dinner

Hook: “Your kid only eats chicken, potatoes, cheddar, and broccoli? Watch this.”

Show the four ingredients, tap the starter or enter them, reveal the recipe, make one swap, and end with the finished plate. Keep the app demonstration visible for at least three seconds.

### 2. Comment-to-recipe

Hook: “Give me four foods you actually eat. I’ll turn one comment into dinner tomorrow.”

Reply to a real comment with a screen recording and cooking result. Never reveal a commenter’s private information.

### 3. One meal, two versions

Hook: “Stop cooking two completely different dinners.”

Show a base meal, then one ingredient swapped for the picky eater. The visual payoff is two similar plates, not a lecture.

### 4. Picky adult, not picky child

Hook: “Picky eating doesn’t magically disappear at 18.”

Demonstrate a low-pressure adult meal and emphasize privacy and control.

### 5. The ingredient veto

Hook: “Love tacos but hate tomatoes? The whole recipe doesn’t have to die.”

Show a swap happening instantly, then the updated instructions.

### 6. Founder build-in-public

Hook: “I’m building the meal app I wish picky households already had.”

Show one improvement, one honest limitation, and one specific request for feedback. Do not pretend an unfinished feature exists.

## Posting formula

- 0–2 seconds: show the problem in large text.
- 2–8 seconds: show familiar ingredients.
- 8–18 seconds: demonstrate generation or swapping.
- 18–28 seconds: show the finished result.
- Final seconds: one action—comment four foods or visit the app.

Avoid long intros, stock-food footage, generic motivational language, and feature lists without a meal payoff.

## Calls to action

- “Comment four foods you actually eat.”
- “Send this to the person who cooks two dinners.”
- “Try your safe foods at foodmyway.app.”
- “Join the first 250 founding members for a one-time $29.”

Use only one call to action per video.

## Measurement

Track each video’s views, 3-second hold, average watch time, completion rate, profile visits, site visits, first recipes, founding interest, and purchases. The winning content is the content that creates activated users and revenue—not merely views.
