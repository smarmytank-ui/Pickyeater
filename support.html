Support

We want Food My Way to make meals easier. During the private beta, support is handled by email.

Email: support@foodmyway.app

Include these details

  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Your device and browser
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain sensitive information

Email preferences

Every founding-access or product-update email must include an unsubscribe link. You may also ask support@foodmyway.app to remove your address from the founding list.

Common fixes

  • Refresh the app once after a new release.
  • Confirm that private browsing is off if recipes do not stay saved.
  • Use the same browser and device to find locally saved beta recipes.
  • Do not clear site data unless you are comfortable removing local recipes and diary entries.

Safety

Food My Way cannot respond to medical emergencies or provide urgent allergy guidance. Contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional when immediate health assistance is needed.
