Common questions

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The private beta stores recipes locally in your browser. Optional cloud accounts are being prepared for the paid release.

Does it order groceries automatically?

No. When grocery commerce is enabled, you will review every matched product and complete any pickup or delivery order on the grocery provider’s site.
