# Food My Way friends-and-family feedback

Share this link: **https://foodmyway.app**

## Copy-and-send invitation

> I’m testing Food My Way, a new app that turns foods you already like into simple, flexible recipes. Would you try it on your phone without me walking you through it? Make one recipe, change one ingredient, and save it. Then send me the answers to the five questions below. Please be blunt—confusion is more useful than compliments right now: https://foodmyway.app

Do not ask friends or family to purchase during ordinary usability testing. Use the free flow first. Offer a controlled paid test only when you have agreed in advance to reimburse the tester and have a reason to validate a new payment change.

## Five questions to ask

1. What did you think the app would do before you tapped anything?
2. Did you successfully create a recipe without help? If not, where did you get stuck?
3. Would you actually make the recipe it gave you? Why or why not?
4. Which feature felt most valuable, and what felt unnecessary or confusing?
5. After using it, would the $29 one-time Founding offer feel worth paying for? What would need to change for the answer to be yes?

## What each tester should try

- Open the site on their normal phone.
- Enter three or four foods they genuinely eat.
- Generate a recipe.
- Swap one ingredient.
- Change the serving count.
- Save the recipe and reopen it from the Recipe Book.
- Look at the Founding offer, but do not purchase unless specifically recruited for a paid test.

## What to record

For each tester, record only:

- device and browser;
- whether they created, swapped, saved, and reopened a recipe;
- whether they would make the result;
- their exact words about confusion and value;
- whether they would pay $29 and why;
- permission status before reusing any quote publicly.

Do not collect medical details, diagnoses, children’s names, payment information, or private recipe content that is not needed for the feedback decision.

## First cohort target

Start with 10 people: a mix of picky adults, parents, and people who cook for a selective household. Aim for at least 8 of 10 to generate a recipe without help and at least 5 of 10 to say they would realistically make the result. Fix repeated confusion before inviting the next 15 testers.
