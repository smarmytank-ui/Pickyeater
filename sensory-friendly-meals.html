Keep the sensory qualities that work for you.

Choose familiar foods, leave unwanted ingredients out, and create meals that respect strong preferences without pretending every substitution is equivalent.

Start with familiar ingredients
You know your experience best.The app follows your ingredient choices. It does not diagnose sensory conditions or promise that a product or recipe will feel safe.

Recipe flexibility for real sensory preferences

A seemingly small substitution can change texture, smell, temperature, appearance, or predictability. Food My Way makes ingredient changes visible and deliberate. Save the exact version that worked instead of relying on a generic recipe again.

Leave-out listPrevent selected foods from appearing in starter ideas and suggestions.
Visible ingredientsReview each component and its quantity before you cook.
Repeatable versionsSave your successful recipe rather than a theoretically similar original.

Use it as a planning tool

  1. Enter foods whose texture and flavor are predictable for you.
  2. Review the proposed preparation and replace anything that does not fit.
  3. Adjust quantities and serving size without changing the whole recipe.
  4. Save the version and use it in a low-pressure weekly plan.

What Food My Way does not claim

Are recipes sensory-safe?

No app can guarantee another person’s sensory response. Food My Way organizes your choices; you decide whether each ingredient and preparation works.

Is this medical or therapeutic guidance?

No. It is not a diagnostic, clinical, feeding-therapy, or emergency tool.
