Food without the judgment

Recipes for picky adults that start with what already works.

You do not need to become an adventurous eater overnight. Name a few familiar foods and Food My Way turns them into a simple recipe you can adjust.

Build from my safe foods
Your preferences are valid.Keep textures, flavors, and ingredients that work. Swap the rest without rebuilding the entire meal.

A practical tool—not a lecture about how you “should” eat

Generic recipe sites often begin with a finished dish and ask you to remove half of it. Food My Way begins with ingredients you choose. That makes it useful for selective eaters, adults with strong texture preferences, people who dislike mixed foods, and anyone exhausted by scrolling through meals they would never make.

Familiar firstBegin with dependable foods instead of unfamiliar recipe suggestions.
Flexible swapsReplace one ingredient while keeping the rest of the meal intact.
No performanceThere is no score for eating broadly and no pressure to “graduate” from safe foods.

How to make a meal your way

  1. Enter a few ingredients you reliably enjoy.
  2. Review a simple recipe with estimated quantities and nutrition.
  3. Swap ingredients, adjust servings, and save the version that works.
  4. Add saved meals to a weekly plan and consolidated grocery list.

Straight answers

Is this nutrition or eating-disorder treatment?

No. Food My Way is a recipe and planning tool, not medical care or treatment. Nutrition is estimated.

Do I have to enter foods I dislike?

No. Start entirely with familiar foods and use the optional leave-out list for ingredients you do not want suggested.
