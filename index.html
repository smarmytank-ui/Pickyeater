            Frequently asked questions

            Is this only for children?

            No. It is for picky adults, parents, neurodivergent households, and anyone who wants less pressure around food.

            Does Food My Way provide medical or allergy advice?

            No. Recipes and nutrition are informational estimates. Always verify labels and speak with a qualified professional about allergies, eating disorders, or medical nutrition needs.

            Where are my recipes stored?

            During the private beta, recipes stay in your browser unless you share them. Cloud accounts and synchronization are being prepared for the premium release.
